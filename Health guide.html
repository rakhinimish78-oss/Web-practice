Building a Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A healthy plate gives our body the energy it needs to study, play, think, and grow. It should include colourful fruits, fresh vegetables, grains, and protein-rich food.

Nutrition Tips

Drink Plenty of Water

Water is essential for our body to function properly. It helps in digestion, absorption of nutrients, and maintaining body temperature. Aim to drink at least 8 glasses of water a day.

Eat a Variety of Foods

Different foods provide different nutrients. Include a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and proteins in your diet to ensure you get all the essential nutrients your body needs.

Limit Sugary Drinks and Snacks

Sugary drinks and snacks can contribute to weight gain and other health issues. Try to limit your intake of these items and opt for healthier alternatives when possible.
